MATERIALS USED

Premium materials designed to return to soil.

Seedling products combine refined texture with regenerative science: compostable plant-based materials, embedded seeds, and mineral support that helps the product become part of a new growth cycle.

Fiber Seed Mineral

WHAT GOES INSIDE

Every layer has a purpose.

Plant-Based Fiber Body

The core structure uses biodegradable fiber blends chosen to feel strong in hand while breaking down naturally when returned to soil or compost-friendly conditions.

Compostable Binding Layer

Instead of traditional plastic coatings, Seedling uses compostable binding support that helps the bag hold its shape during use and soften during decomposition.

Embedded Seed Capsules

Discreet capsules are placed within the product architecture so that, after use, the product carries the possibility of sprouting into a mystery plant.

Nourishing Minerals

Mineral support is included to enrich the surrounding soil as the product breaks down, helping create a better early environment for seed activation.

Low-Impact Finishes

Visual finishes are selected to preserve Seedling's premium look while keeping the product aligned with its clean-return purpose.

Soil-Return Design

The full material system is designed around the final step: tear, place in soil, keep lightly moist, and allow nature to begin the transformation.

WHY IT MATTERS

Less waste, more renewal.

Conventional disposable packaging often ends its life as long-lasting waste. Seedling materials are chosen for a different ending: they are made to disappear cleanly, return useful matter to the ground, and support the possibility of new plant life.
