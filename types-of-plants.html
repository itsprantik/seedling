INDIAN PLANT COLLECTION

Plants that feel meaningful, useful, and beautiful.

Tulsi

Known as holy basil, Tulsi is cherished in Indian homes for its fragrance, resilience, and cultural meaning.

Jasmine

A delicate flowering plant with luminous white blossoms and a soft scent often associated with celebration.

Marigold

Bright orange and gold flowers that bring warmth to gardens, festivals, and pollinator-friendly spaces.

Lotus

An elegant aquatic bloom, admired across India as a symbol of renewal, purity, and quiet strength.

Hibiscus

A bold tropical flower with rich color, glossy leaves, and a dramatic presence in warm gardens.

Neem

A hardy native tree valued for shade, endurance, and its long role in traditional Indian living.

Curry Leaf

A fragrant kitchen plant whose leaves bring fresh, unmistakable aroma to Indian cooking.

Aparajita

Also called butterfly pea, this climbing plant grows striking blue flowers that feel almost jewel-like.
