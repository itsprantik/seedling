HOW IT WORKS

From a torn bag to a living plant.

Seedling bags are designed for a second life. When the bag is torn, damaged, or has finished its use, it can be returned to soil where its biodegradable body releases nutrients and embedded seeds begin the growth cycle.

THE REGENERATION PROCESS

A simple ritual with a beautiful finish.

01

Use the bag normally

Carry groceries, gifts, daily essentials, or event items just like you would with a premium carry bag. The material is crafted to feel refined while remaining compostable.

02

Let it finish its purpose

Once the bag becomes torn, worn out, damaged, or no longer usable, it is ready for its second role. Instead of throwing it into ordinary waste, prepare it for soil.

03

Place it into soil

Tear the bag into smaller pieces if possible, then bury it under healthy soil or place it into a compost-friendly planting area. Keep the soil lightly moist so breakdown can begin.

04

The material breaks down

The biodegradable bag softens and decomposes through moisture, soil contact, and natural microbial activity. As it breaks apart, it returns useful organic matter back into the earth.

05

Nutrients and seeds activate

Inside the bag are discreet seed capsules and nourishing minerals. As the bag decomposes, these capsules are released into the soil, giving the seed a supportive starting environment.

06

A plant begins to grow

With the right sunlight, moisture, air, and soil conditions, the embedded seed can sprout into a mystery plant. The exact growth depends on your local climate and care.

WHAT IS INSIDE

Designed with seed, nutrients, and compostable material.

The bag is not just packaging. It is a small regenerative system. The outer body is made from biodegradable compounds that can return to soil, while the inner seed capsules carry plant potential. Nutrient-rich mineral support helps create a better early environment for germination once the bag is placed in soil.

Compostable body

Breaks down naturally instead of remaining as long-lasting plastic waste.

Embedded seeds

Hidden inside the bag so every finished product carries the chance of new life.

Nourishing minerals

Support the soil around the capsule as the plant begins its first stage of growth.

For best results

Use loose, healthy soil with drainage.

Bury pieces shallowly, around 1 to 2 inches deep.

Keep the soil lightly moist, not flooded.

Place the pot or soil bed where it receives suitable sunlight.
